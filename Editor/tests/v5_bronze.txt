# V5 (docs/VISION_MUNDO_VIVO.md): an era is a pack of data. era2.pack.json adds to era1 the Bronze Age (its key technology, alloying), tin, bronze,
# a need of the age (faith) and its verb (pray), done at a building that provides it (the temple): no code knows any of them. The clan is given
# smelting and alloying, so it is in the Bronze Age; its people lose faith (a test's copy of the need that falls in seconds) and go to pray at the
# temple. The era is state: it is saved and loaded with the clan.
pack load era2 strict
generate temperate 5
resources world
site best
tribe build fox
content load v5_faith_fast.json
knowledge grant clan smelting
knowledge grant clan alloying
settlement raise temple
wait 600
qa expect "village report" "Bronze Age"
wait 1200
qa expect "tribe log 80" "prays at Temple 1 \(Faith\)"
save v5_bronze
load v5_bronze
qa expect "village report" "Bronze Age"
quit
