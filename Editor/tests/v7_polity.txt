# town, city, by people and finished buildings); a rank reached is history ("Clan 1 become a village"); a building may ask for a rank (the town
# hall of a town). Clans bound by alliances are a polity, named after the biggest; one that forms is history too. No player: nobody's death
# sets off the leap between lives.
