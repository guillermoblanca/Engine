# H13: two clans in one world. The first tribe is clan 0; `clan new` places another with the site chooser away from it. They live side by side, the
# game is saved and loaded with both (the "clans" section), and the second clan comes back with its members.
generate temperate 5
resources world 0.5
tribe build fox 0 0 4
clan new Blue 4
clan list
timescale 10
wait 300
clan relations
clan territory
save clans_h13
load clans_h13
clan select Blue
tribe status
clan list
quit
