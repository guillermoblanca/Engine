# H3 demo (principle 7): the interactive world and its ecology. The tribe lights its campfire (the Fire Pit interactive) and a member stokes it with
# wood from the store; rabbits and two foxes live beside the camp and the foxes hunt. The invariants hold (the fire is lit exactly when it has fuel),
# (the foxes come hungry: h3_hungry_fox.json, a copy of the fox of world.json with spawnFood 0.45, so the hunt starts at once),
# and a screenshot of the World state panel is written to the build directory. A failed line fails the test (--strict=1).
generate temperate 5
quality minimum
content load h3_hungry_fox.json
tribe build fox 0 0 4
ecology spawn 6 12 8
ecology spawn 2 12 8 fox
timescale 20
wait 400
# (the fire is lit within the first two minutes of game; the tribe's log keeps only its latest lines, so it is read while they are still in it)
qa expect "tribe log 100000" "lights the campfire"
qa expect "tribe log 100000" "feeds the campfire"
wait 1100
qa expect "ecology status" "caught by hunters [1-9]"
qa check
panel WorldState on
wait 3
shot_ui h3_fire_fox.png
quit
