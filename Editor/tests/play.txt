# H15: the game mode, headless. `play` makes a world, two clans (the site chooser places them) and the player as a member of clan 0 (the X Bot of
# packs/full, or the fox without it); some in-game minutes pass with the player acting through `game` commands, as its keys would: it claims a job
# of the board, takes berries from the store and eats them, proposes work to the clan (a newcomer is turned down) and saves; the game is loaded and
# the player is still the clan's, with its standing and its proposal; the HUD says so (and a capture of it is left in the build directory,
# play_hud.png) and the invariants of the simulation hold. A failed line fails the test (--strict=1).
play temperate 5 3 2
qa expect "game status" "player: Player \(forager\); standing 10"
qa expect "game status" "needs: Hunger [0-9]+ Thirst"
qa expect "game status" "condition: health"
qa expect "tribe status" "Player"
# (the test runs with --step=60: a frame is 1/60 s of game, so a wait is the same stretch of game on any machine. The first berries reach the
# store at about 95 s of game: 330 frames at timescale 20 are 110 s)
timescale 20
wait 330
timescale 10
game claim
game clan
game take berry 2
game eat
qa expect "game status" "carries: berry"
wait 300
game propose gather wood 10
game help
wait 300
qa expect "game status" "time: day [0-9]+"
qa expect "tribe log 400" "Player \(forager\) eats|takes 2 berry"
shot_ui play_hud.png
game save h15_play
game load h15_play
wait 5
qa expect "game status" "player: Player \(forager\); standing [0-9]+"
qa expect "tribe jobs" "proposal #1"
qa check
qa expect "qa status" "[1-9][0-9]* live checks, 0 violation"
quit
