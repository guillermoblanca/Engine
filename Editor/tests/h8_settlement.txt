# H8 demo (principle 7): on a generated world with wild resource nodes the site choice is asked and the clan is founded where it says (no patch: the
# wild nodes are enough); a construction site is put down, the clan carries the materials and works it, the game is saved and loaded in the middle of
# the work and the site comes back; the invariants hold (the "buildings" one included). A failed line fails the test (--strict=1).
generate temperate 5
quality minimum
resources world
site best
site list 3
tribe build fox
qa expect "tribe status" "wild resource nodes|store:"
settlement buildings
settlement start shelter
qa expect "settlement status" "Shelter 1 \(shelter\): building the foundation"
timescale 20
wait 120
save h8_settlement
load h8_settlement
qa expect "settlement status" "Shelter 1 \(shelter\)"
wait 30
qa check
qa expect "qa status" "[1-9][0-9]* live checks, 0 violation"
quit
