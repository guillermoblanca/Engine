# V8 (docs/VISION_MUNDO_VIVO.md): one pack of data per era, from the Bronze Age to the arrival of AI (era2..era9.pack.json, each needing the one
# before). Loaded strictly, the check finds every item, recipe, node, technology and building can be had; and a clan that learns the key of each
# era is in that era, in the order of history (the eras a pack adds go right after the eras they follow).
pack load era9 strict
qa expect "pack load era9 strict" "0 problem"
