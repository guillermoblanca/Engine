# age, couples, births: newborns get their bodies), a next step learnt every 50 years, all written to the history as one event that every member
# knows. The player dies of old age and the world leaps the years of its era (era1.json: the Stone Age leaps 500); then an heir is chosen
# among those living, who remembers them; the game is saved and loaded with them.
