# V0 (docs/VISION_MUNDO_VIVO.md): the player can be anyone of any clan. `play` makes a world with two clans and the player in the first; `game become
# clan Blue` takes over the first member of the second clan: the character left goes back to its clan's brain and the HUD, the orders and the store
# are now the Blue clan's. The game is saved and loaded and the player is still that member of the Blue (a member marked as a player in a kept clan).
play temperate 5 3 2
qa expect "game status" "player: Player \(forager\)"
timescale 10
wait 120
game become clan Blue
qa expect "game status" "player: (?!Player )[A-Z][a-z]+ \("
qa expect "game take berry 1" "store is at the camp|you take"
wait 120
qa expect "clan list" "Blue"
game save v0_become
game load v0_become
qa expect "game status" "player: (?!Player )[A-Z][a-z]+ \("
game become Player
qa expect "game status" "player: Player \("
qa check
qa expect "qa status" "[1-9][0-9]* live checks, 0 violation"
quit
